Research Digest

An offline-first companion for keeping up with research.

This is a progressive web app you can install to your devices. It fetches papers from arXiv, then tags them and pulls out their key sentences with tiny offline DistilBERT models running entirely on your own device — no API keys, no cloud, no account. Free forever.

Open the app Contribute / file issues
  • Fetching, scoring, and summarization all run in your browser.
  • Your interests and saved papers stay on this device — no account, nothing synced.
  • Works offline after your first visit.
